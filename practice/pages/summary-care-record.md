Your GP practice keeps your patient record securely. The Summary Care Record makes some information from your GP record available to authorised health professionals involved in your care in other NHS settings in England, such as an emergency department or minor injury unit.

The information is used by authorised health professionals directly involved in your care. They should ask your permission before accessing it unless they cannot ask and there is a clinical reason to access it.

## Information in the record

The Summary Care Record includes:

- your current medicines
- allergies
- bad reactions to medicines

Your GP may add other information when you request it.

## Opting out

Ask reception for an opt-out form. A parent or guardian can request that a child under 16 opts out. The GP must consider the child's understanding and the duty of care to the child.

For more information, read the [NHS England Digital guide to the Summary Care Record](https://digital.nhs.uk/services/summary-care-records-scr/summary-care-records-scr-information-for-patients).
