## Request an appointment

Use the **Medical Issue** option in Accurx for a new or ongoing health problem. Online medical requests are available from **7:30am to 6:30pm on working days**. A GP triages medical requests and the practice provides a response within one working day.

The response may be an appointment or a referral to a more appropriate service. Appointments may be by phone, face to face, video, text or email, depending on your request.

## Admin help

Choose **Admin Query** for administrative requests, including fit notes. Do not submit a medical problem through the admin option. The practice team reviews admin requests and aims to contact you within one working day.

If the service is closed or you cannot use it, call [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538). Reception can submit a request on your behalf, and staff can also help you at the surgery.

## Cancel an appointment

Use the cancellation link in your appointment reminder text. If your appointment is within the next 12 hours and you have not received a text, call reception.

For other appointments, you can also use the **Admin Request** option in [Accurx](https://accurx.nhs.uk/patient-initiated/p84045). If you cannot cancel online, call [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538) and use the automated **Check and Cancel** service, or speak to reception.

## Home visits

If you are housebound and need a home visit, call the practice as early as possible on the day. A GP will assess whether there is sufficient medical need for a visit.

## Evening and weekend appointments

Extended hours appointments are available on Tuesday and Friday evenings and some Saturdays, at The Park Medical Centre and other local surgeries. Ask reception which appointments are available. These are routine appointments, not an emergency service.
