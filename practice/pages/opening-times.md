## Extended hours

Evening appointments are available on Tuesday and Friday. Some Saturday appointments are also available through the extended access service. Contact reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538) for appointment times and locations.

The practice closes for staff training on some afternoons. Please contact reception before travelling if you need to check a closure.

## Online requests

Online medical requests are available from 7:30am to 6:30pm on working days. [Request an appointment or admin help](/appointments/).

## When we are closed

Use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111) for urgent help when the practice is closed. Call [999](tel:999) in a life-threatening emergency.
