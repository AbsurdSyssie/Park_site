**1 September 2026**

The August 2026 Friends and Family Test received 50 responses. Of these, 47 people (94%) said their experience was good or very good.

![August 2026 Friends and Family results chart: 47 of 50 responses were good or very good (94%).](/images/news/august-2026-friends-and-family.png)

Patients could respond by text message or by using a paper card at reception.
