Use the NHS App to order repeat prescriptions and view your medical records and test results.

[Find out about the NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/)

Use the app to check [test results](/test-results/) and manage your [contact details](/update-your-details/). You can also [find your NHS number](/find-your-nhs-number/) through the NHS service.

To request an appointment at The Park Medical Centre, use the [practice's appointment service](/appointments/). If you need help using online services, call reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538).
