Some services are not covered by our contract with the NHS and therefore have a charge. Examples include:

- Medicals for pre-employment, sports and driving requirements (including HGV and PSV)
- Insurance claim forms
- Prescriptions for taking medication abroad
- Private sick notes
- Vaccination certificates

Fees are based on the British Medical Association (BMA) suggested scales. Reception staff can advise you about fees and appointment availability. Contact reception for further information.
