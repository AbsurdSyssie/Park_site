## Order a repeat prescription

You can order repeat prescriptions:

- using the NHS App
- in writing, by ticking the items you need on your repeat prescription slip or writing a request
- by dropping your written request at the surgery or sending it by post

{{OFFICIAL_PRESCRIPTION_ACTION_LINK}}

**Telephone prescription orders are not accepted.**

Allow **three full working days** before collecting your prescription. If ordering by post, allow at least seven days. Include a stamped, self-addressed envelope if you need the prescription posted back to you.

## Medication questions

For a medication query, use the online request service. If you cannot use the service, contact reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538) for help.

## Electronic prescriptions

The practice can send prescriptions electronically to your chosen pharmacy. Ask reception to sign you up for the Electronic Prescription Service.

[Find a pharmacy](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/pharmacy/find-a-pharmacy/)

## Medication reviews

Patients on repeat medication are asked to attend a review with a doctor, nurse practitioner or practice nurse at least once a year. Your repeat prescription slip will tell you when a review is due. Please book in advance when a review is required.

[Read about medicines on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/)
