For practical information about what to do after someone dies, read the [GOV.UK guidance](https://www.gov.uk/after-a-death).

You can also contact these bereavement support organisations:

- [Cruse Bereavement Support](https://www.cruse.org.uk/get-help/)
- [Hope Again, support for young people](https://www.hopeagain.org.uk/)
- [Winston's Wish, support for bereaved children and young people](https://winstonswish.org/)
- [Childhood Bereavement Network](https://childhoodbereavementnetwork.org.uk/)
