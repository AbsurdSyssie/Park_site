Your views help the practice understand what is working well and what can be improved. You can leave a public [Google review](https://g.page/r/CQMcwApOdW8-EAE/review), or share feedback privately with the practice.

## Friends and Family Test

Tell us how likely you are to recommend the practice by completing the [Friends and Family Test](https://forms.office.com/e/Gh4WZfmkC0). This form is hosted by Microsoft Forms.

## Other ways to give feedback

- Call reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538) or speak to a member of the practice team.
- Complete a feedback form at reception.
- Join the [Patient Participation Group](/patient-participation-group/) to contribute to practice discussions.
- If you have a concern or complaint, see [how to make a complaint](/complaints/).
