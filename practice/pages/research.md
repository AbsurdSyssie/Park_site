Discover Me UK is a nationwide research study about how health and genetic information can help researchers understand disease. The study is open to people aged 18 or over who can give consent, where their GP practice or online patient service provider takes part.

Check the [Discover Me UK participating practices page](https://www.discovermestudy.com/dmuk/practices) to see whether The Park Medical Centre is taking part.
