title: Self-referrals and referral tracking
---

Most specialist referrals need a discussion with a GP. Some local services also accept self-referrals. Check each service's own referral arrangements. The practice lists these services:

- [Maternity services at Saint Mary's Hospital](https://mft.nhs.uk/saint-marys/services/maternity-services-obstetrics/)
- [Northern Contraception, Sexual Health and HIV Service](https://mft.nhs.uk/mri/services/956-2/)
- [Carers Manchester](https://www.carersmanchester.org.uk/)
- [42nd Street, support for young people's mental health](https://www.42ndstreet.org.uk/)
- [Urgent dental care from gtd healthcare](https://www.gtdhealthcare.co.uk/patient-services/dental)

For help finding a service, contact reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538).

## Track a referral

If you have been referred for specialist care, the Care Gateway Team will send you a letter explaining how to book and any passwords you need.

For an update on your referral, call **[0161&nbsp;947&nbsp;0770](tel:01619470770)**.

[Care Gateway information](https://gmintegratedcare.org.uk/care-gateway/)
