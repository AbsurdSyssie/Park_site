## GP earnings for 2024/25

The practice reported average GP earnings before tax and National Insurance of **£100,928** for **0 full-time GPs, 3 part-time GPs and 1 locum GP** who worked at the practice for more than six months.

This figure does not account for how much time each doctor worked at the practice and should not be used to compare individual GPs' earnings.
