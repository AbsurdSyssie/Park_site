**1 September 2026**

The practice’s August 2026 update shared figures about patient numbers, calls, appointments, online requests, clinical documents and home visits.

![The Park Medical Centre's August 2026 activity poster. The figures are also listed below.](/images/news/august-2026-activity.png)

### August figures

- Registered patients: 5,734
- Average telephone queue time: 4 minutes 34 seconds
- Incoming calls: 1,797
- Appointments not attended: 141
- GP and advanced nurse practitioner appointments: 1,208
- Practice nurse and specialist diabetic nurse appointments: 82
- Physician assistant appointments: 205
- Healthcare assistant appointments: 209
- Online medical requests: 1,282
- Online admin requests: 697
- Clinical documents received and actioned: 2,008
- Home visits: 23
