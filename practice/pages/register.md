## Join The Park Medical Centre

{{OFFICIAL_REGISTER_ACTION_LINK}}

You can register yourself or someone else online. Paper forms are also available at reception. You do not need proof of address, immigration status, ID or an NHS number to register.

If you have any questions, call [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538).

## Practice area

Contact reception to check the practice area before registering. The practice also welcomes out-of-area registrations within Greater Manchester, subject to conditions:

- You must be able to travel to the surgery for face-to-face appointments, reviews, immunisations and screening.
- You must respond to invitations and attend appointments, including appointments for children.
- Home visits are only available within the local area. If you become housebound outside the area, you will need to register closer to home.

Ask reception about the out-of-area agreement.

## Temporary registration

Contact reception if you need temporary registration while away from home.

## After registration

The practice will contact you when your registration is approved. You will be allocated an accountable GP, but you can still see other clinicians at the practice.
