The Park Medical Centre provides NHS GP services from 434 Altrincham Road, Baguley, Wythenshawe, Manchester, M23 9AB.

The practice is operated by an unlimited partnership. The partners are Dr Shahla Hannan, Dr Suman Sarin and Dr Priyanka Vaidya. [See the practice team](/our-team/).

## Care Quality Commission

The practice's overall CQC rating is **Good**.

[Read the CQC report for The Park Medical Centre](https://www.cqc.org.uk/location/1-571476646)

[View the practice's NHS profile](https://www.nhs.uk/services/gp-surgery/the-park-medical-centre/P84045)

## GP earnings

The practice's published declaration for **2024/25** states average GP earnings of **£100,928**, before tax and National Insurance, for **3 part-time GPs and 1 locum GP** who worked at the practice for more than six months.

[Read the practice's GP earnings declaration](https://www.parkmedicalcentrewythenshawe.nhs.uk/gp-earnings)
