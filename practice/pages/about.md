The practice's overall CQC rating is **Good**.

## GP earnings

The practice's published declaration for **2024/25** states average GP earnings of **£100,928**, before tax and National Insurance, for **3 part-time GPs and 1 locum GP** who worked at the practice for more than six months.

[Read the practice's GP earnings declaration](https://www.parkmedicalcentrewythenshawe.nhs.uk/gp-earnings)
