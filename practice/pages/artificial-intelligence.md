Artificial intelligence can support healthcare professionals, but it does not replace your doctor or nurse. Healthcare professionals check and approve any AI-generated output.

You can opt out of AI use without affecting your care. Dr Vaidya oversees AI safety at the practice. The practice uses NHS-approved AI systems.
