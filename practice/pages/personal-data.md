The practice uses these electronic systems to support your care and manage your records:

- referral management, which uses your NHS number in referrals
- online booking and cancellation of routine appointments
- online repeat prescription requests
- the Summary Care Record, which can make your current medicines and allergies available to clinicians involved in your care elsewhere
- electronic transfers of your records when you register with another GP practice
- online access to your medical records

To ask about online access to your records or to opt out of the local or national Summary Care Record, contact reception on [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538).

Read the [privacy notice](/privacy-notice/) for information about how the practice handles your personal data.
