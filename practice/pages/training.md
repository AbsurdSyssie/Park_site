## GP training

The practice trains qualified doctors who want to specialise in general practice. GP registrars have experience as hospital doctors and consult patients under the mentorship of the practice trainer, Dr Vaidya.

The practice may ask permission to record a consultation for education. Staff will ask you in advance. You can decline, and this will not affect your care. The practice will not record a consultation without your consent. You can ask for the camera to be switched off. Recordings are used for education with the doctor who carried out the consultation and are destroyed afterwards.

## Medical students

Medical students may be placed at the practice for 4 to 6 weeks as part of their training. Tell the receptionist if you do not want a student to be present during your consultation.
