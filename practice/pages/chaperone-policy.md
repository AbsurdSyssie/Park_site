At The Park Medical Centre, we respect your privacy, dignity and cultural beliefs, especially during intimate examinations. We will only carry out an intimate examination with your explicit agreement. You can ask to have a chaperone present.

## Requesting a chaperone

You can request a chaperone:

- when booking your appointment by telling the receptionist
- when you arrive at the surgery by telling the reception team
- at any time during your consultation

## Confidentiality

Chaperones follow the practice's confidentiality policy. They must not discuss details of an appointment they have chaperoned with other staff members.
