Practice staff must treat patients and their representatives with respect and courtesy. We ask patients and their representatives to do the same when dealing with the practice team.

The NHS has a zero-tolerance policy towards violence and abuse. The practice may remove a patient from its list to protect staff, patients and other people.

Aggressive behaviour, whether verbal or physical, may be reported to the police and may lead to removal from the practice list. Violence includes actual or threatened physical violence and verbal abuse that makes someone fear for their safety. If the practice removes a patient from its list, it will notify them in writing and record the circumstances in their medical record.
