## Name and contact details

The practice has no fax number.

## Partnership and team

The practice is operated as an unlimited partnership. The partners are Dr Shahla Hannan, Dr Suman Sarin and Dr Priyanka Vaidya. See [the practice team](/our-team/) for their primary medical qualifications and GMC numbers, and for the wider team list.

## GP and medical student training

The practice trains doctors who want to specialise in general practice, and medical students may be placed at the practice. See [training at the practice](/training/) for information about consultations and recordings.

## Practice area

Contact reception to check whether your address is in the practice area. The practice also accepts some out-of-area registrations within Greater Manchester. You must be able to travel to the surgery for in-person appointments and reviews. Home visits are only available within the local area. If you become housebound outside that area, you will need to register with a practice nearer to your home. Read the [registration information](/register/) and [out-of-area agreement](/images/documents/new-patient-out-of-area-agreement.pdf), or [view the practice boundary and check your postcode](https://nhs-gp-catchment.scwcsu.nhs.uk/catchment-embed/?odscode=P84045).

## Access to the premises

There are ramps at the front and rear of the building and a powered door at the front. There is parking, including disabled parking, wheelchair access to all areas, accessible toilets and an induction loop at the front desk. See [contact and access information](/contact/).

## Registering with the practice

You can register online or collect a paper form from reception. You do not need proof of address, immigration status, identity or an NHS number to register. Temporary registration is also available. See [how to register](/register/).

## Choosing a healthcare professional

You can express a preference about the healthcare professional or type of professional you would like to see, generally or for a particular condition. The practice will record your preference and try to meet it where reasonable. A particular professional may not provide the service you need or may have reasonable grounds to decline. Waiting longer may be necessary to see your preferred professional. See [how to request an appointment](/appointments/).

## Services

The practice provides GP and nursing services, including clinics for asthma, COPD, coronary heart disease, diabetes, smoking cessation and weight management. Other services include family planning, cervical screening, minor surgery, joint injection clinics and blood tests. Read the full [services and support information](/services/).

## Opening hours and contact

The practice is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6:30pm. It is closed for routine reception on Saturdays and closed on Sundays. Evening appointments are available on Tuesdays and Fridays, with some Saturday appointments. Online medical requests are available on working days from 7:30am to 6:30pm. See [full opening times](/opening-times/) and [contact details](/contact/).

## Home visits

If you are vulnerable or housebound and there is sufficient medical need, call the practice as early as possible on the day. A GP may visit if there is sufficient medical evidence that you cannot attend the surgery. Read the [appointments and home visit information](/appointments/).

## Consultations under the regulations

Patients aged 16 to 74 can request a consultation if they have not had a consultation or attended a practice clinic in the previous three years. Patients aged 75 and over can request a consultation if they have not had one in the previous 12 months. This does not mean the practice will automatically invite patients for a consultation.

For a patient aged 75 or over, the practice will arrange a home consultation if a medical condition makes attending the surgery or having a remote consultation inappropriate. The practice assesses this based on the patient's circumstances. These arrangements are described in [Schedule 3, paragraphs 9 and 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1862/schedule/3).


## When the practice is closed

For urgent help when the practice is closed, use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111). Call [999](tel:999) in a life-threatening emergency. See [urgent help](/urgent-help/).

## Repeat prescriptions

Order repeat prescriptions using the NHS App or in writing. The practice does not accept prescription orders by telephone. Allow three full working days before collection, or at least seven days for a postal request. Electronic prescriptions can be sent to your chosen pharmacy. See [how to order prescriptions](/prescriptions/).

The practice is not a dispensing practice.

## Feedback and complaints

You can share feedback by calling reception, speaking to a team member, completing a form at reception or joining the Patient Participation Group. If you have a concern about care or service, contact reception and ask for the Practice Manager. Read [feedback options](/feedback/) or the full [complaints process](/complaints/).

## Rights and responsibilities

You have the right to be treated with dignity and respect, to have your privacy respected and to be involved in decisions about your care. The NHS Constitution explains your rights and responsibilities. Please arrive on time, cancel appointments you cannot attend as soon as possible, give accurate information and treat staff and other patients respectfully. Read [You and Your General Practice](https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/you-and-your-general-practice-english/) and the [NHS Constitution for England](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-nhs-constitution-for-england/the-nhs-constitution-for-england).
