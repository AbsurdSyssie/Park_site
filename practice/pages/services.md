## Practice nurse clinics

The practice nurse runs clinics for asthma, COPD, coronary heart disease, diabetes, smoking cessation and weight management.

## Family planning and cervical screening

The family planning clinic offers coils and implants. Contact reception for appointment information and cervical screening appointments.

## Minor surgery and joint injections

Ask reception about minor surgery and joint injection clinics.

## Blood tests

A phlebotomy clinic is held on Tuesday mornings, excluding bank holidays. Ask reception or call [0161&nbsp;998&nbsp;5538](tel:01619985538) to book.

## Non-NHS services

Some work is not covered by the NHS and may attract a charge. Examples include employment or driving medicals, insurance claim forms, private sick notes and vaccination certificates. Ask reception about fees and availability before requesting private work.

## Veterans

The Park Medical Centre is an accredited Veteran Friendly practice. Please let reception know if you have served in the Armed Forces so your records can be updated.

[NHS healthcare for the Armed Forces community](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/armed-forces-community/)
