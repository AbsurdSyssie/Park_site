The practice holds personal and medical information so we can provide care. Please tell us when your details change so we can keep your records up to date.

## Confidentiality and personal information

Staff access your records only to do their work. We may share information with healthcare professionals involved in your care. All staff receive confidentiality training and have legal and contractual obligations when handling information.

## Keeping information secure

The practice uses safeguards to protect your information from accidental loss. We verify identity before disclosing information by telephone or fax. We do not share your information with family, friends or spouses without your prior written consent. We do not leave messages about personal details with other people.

## Legal disclosures and reports

The law may require us to share information with statutory or official bodies, for example under a court order or for a public health purpose. We need your written consent for some reports, such as medical reports for insurers or solicitors.

## Access to your records

You can request access to your medical records. Contact reception for information about the process. An appointment may be needed and a fee may apply in some cases.

Read the [privacy notice](/privacy-notice/) for more information about how the practice handles personal information.
