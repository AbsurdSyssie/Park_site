Your privacy and confidentiality matter to us. The practice handles your information under NHS policies and UK data protection law.

## How we protect your information

Healthcare professionals involved in your care can access your medical records. Staff follow data protection rules when handling your personal information.

We share information with hospitals, specialists and other healthcare professionals involved in your care when needed. We may also share information where the law requires it or for urgent medical treatment.

## Your rights

You can ask to:

- see your medical records
- correct information that is inaccurate
- control how and with whom your information is shared
