The practice takes reasonable steps to protect information used by its computer systems.

## Confidentiality

The practice limits system access to authorised people and does not disclose information to people who have no right to see it.

## Accuracy and integrity

Staff take care when entering information, report changes and check the correct record before updating it. The practice monitors changes and investigates apparent errors.

## Availability and security

The practice protects equipment from intruders, takes backups at planned intervals and maintains and tests contingency plans for equipment failure or theft.

The policy refers to the Data Protection Act 2018, the Health and Safety at Work Act 1992 and the Access to Health Records Act 1990.
